Hardware setup

Detect the two serial buses before calibration. Automatic marker calibration requires verified camera coverage and a motor-power emergency stop; it is not yet available. The installed upstream tools also provide hand-guided calibration; this does not complete marker-based automatic calibration.

Motion requires measured load/current limits for this robot. These readings indicate motor effort; they do not measure contact force.

Controller instructions and button map

Hold Start to move; release it to hold position. Back ends the session. Sticks move the arms, bumpers change pitch/wrist control, triggers close grippers, and trigger + stick press opens them. D-pad movement is disabled.

Recording backups

Original recordings and immutable local snapshots are retained. Remote copies are marked verified only after the receiver checks every sample against its SHA-256 manifest. Interrupted recordings remain labelled incomplete even when their preserved bytes are verified.

Leave the token blank to keep the saved token for this destination. Clear the URL to disable remote copies while retaining all existing recordings and backups.
