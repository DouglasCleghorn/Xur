Keep the hardware.
Change what it does.

A profile describes which workloads to run and where they belong. Save a few for the way you use your machine, then switch from the control panel.

Give each workload its space

Assign GPUs to desktops, models and containers. Independent workloads load in parallel; a failure in one does not discard the others.

Keep your desktop familiar

Reuse a named workstation across profiles to keep its user and Moonlight pairing. Assign USB devices or a hub, and choose a primary desktop for shared peripherals.

Use your models

Find ready language-model endpoints in one place. Try a conversation or retain a benchmark with the model, running workloads and sampled VRAM.
